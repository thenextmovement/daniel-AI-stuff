- The 233 executions collapsed into 43 stable historical fingerprints.

- Largest repeated fingerprint: Quote Ready Size Ladder Gate credential failure, 58 executions.

## Remaining cutover gate

The seven-day wait is removed. Do not disable legacy email until the router has a deterministic notification step that:

1. sends the first occurrence of a technical fingerprint immediately;
2. sends at most one reminder per fingerprint per 24 hours;
3. leaves the 52 custom/critical business warnings unchanged;
4. fails open to a notification if state lookup is unavailable;
5. has an idempotent send/outbox key so an ambiguous Outlook result cannot duplicate mail;
6. preserves the dated full-JSON rollback.
