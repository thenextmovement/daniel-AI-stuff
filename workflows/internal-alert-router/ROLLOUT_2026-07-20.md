## Seven-day gate

Do not disable legacy email before all conditions pass:

1. At least seven consecutive calendar days of shadow data.
2. Router writes succeed for at least 99% of attempted alerts.
3. Every legacy hard-error email is represented by a router execution or an explicitly recorded shadow-write failure.
4. No recursive alert chain.
5. Duplicate fingerprints are manually sampled and confirmed to represent the same actionable incident.
6. Critical and first-occurrence alerts retain an immediate notification path.
7. Rollback from the dated full JSON snapshots is rehearsed or reviewed.
