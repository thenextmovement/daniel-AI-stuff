# EU-Supplier-Recherche, 24.07.2026

Public web research is a screening aid, not proof of production quality. Sparse reviews are marked as uncertainty. The OPS recommendation should increasingly use verified NEONTRIP metrics: delivery success, response time, quoted versus invoiced cost, lead-time accuracy, defects and rework.

## Recommendation

1. Preferred pilot group: Neon-Light, Goleks and Luminescence.
2. Qualified pilot: DAD.
3. Backup pending stronger evidence: Top Advert.

Do not award all work to one supplier yet. Run comparable pilot orders, verify electrical documentation, warranty, packaging, Incoterms and delivery accuracy, then use the internal score.

## Evidence

### Neon-Light, Poland

- Own site: manufacturer for Poland/Western Europe, channel letters, lightboxes and large-format structures; company registry identifiers and address are published.
- Independent signal: ProvenExpert aggregates 45 Google Maps ratings at 4.5/5. This is the strongest public review volume found in the current set, but the underlying reviews still require spot-checking.
- Recommendation: preferred pilot; comparatively strong public footprint.
- Sources: https://neon-light.eu/ and https://www.provenexpert.com/neon-light/

### Goleks, Latvia

- Own site says founded in 1991, focuses on illuminated/non-illuminated signage and resellers, and reports more than 2,000 square metres of production facilities.
- LinkedIn company profile is consistent on establishment, Riga location and signage specialization, but is not independent quality evidence.
- No robust independent review corpus was found.
- Recommendation: preferred pilot because of manufacturing history and reseller fit, with quality confirmed through sample orders.
- Sources: https://www.goleks.com/ and https://www.goleks.com/about-us and https://www.linkedin.com/company/goleks

### Luminescence, Estonia

- Own site identifies registry/VAT data, illuminated-signage specialization, in-house production/installation and claims ISO 9001/14001.
- The legal entity is young (site says premium manufacturer since 2022) while the team claims experience since 2006; these are different claims and must not be conflated.
- No relevant independent customer-review corpus was found. A similarly named Trustpilot fashion company is unrelated and excluded.
- Recommendation: preferred pilot due to relevant capability and clear quoting; verify certificates and performance through orders.
- Sources: https://www.luminescence.ee/ and https://www.luminescence.ee/about

### DAD, Lithuania

- Own site says operating since 2011 and lists membership in the Baltic Sign Makers Association and European Sign Federation.
- Testimonials on its own site are useful references but not independent reviews.
- No robust independent review corpus was found.
- Recommendation: qualified pilot; verify membership and lead-time performance.
- Source: https://dad.lt/

### Top Advert, Czechia

- Own site says founded in 2008, around 30 staff, direct import and own printing/development; portfolio includes 3D advertising.
- Firmy.cz lists the same address/company identifier and only one rating. One rating is too little for a reliability conclusion.
- Recommendation: retain as backup/pilot, but do not prefer solely from public ratings.
- Sources: https://www.topadvert.cz/en/o-nas/ and https://www.firmy.cz/detail/2333576-top-advert-hodonin.html

## Internal scoring proposal

After at least three completed offers/orders, score: quote completeness 15%, response time 10%, landed price 20%, production accuracy 15%, shipping accuracy 15%, defect/rework rate 20%, communication 5%. Public research is a qualification flag only and must not dominate verified NEONTRIP experience.
