## Review Focus
- Same-day missed inquiry then confirmed offer: stage1 red, stage2 due.
- Offer sent mid-call: outcome remains attached to the stage captured at call start.
- Concurrent operators: optimistic revision conflict prevents overwrite; repeat action id prevents duplicate history.
- Midnight/DST/weekends: calendar offsets Berlin, overdue items retained.
- Ignore and closed requests: viewing/resending cannot resurrect; missing scores remain unknown.
