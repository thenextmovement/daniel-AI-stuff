About NEONTRIP – LED Signage from Düsseldorf, Germany

NEONTRIP is a German manufacturer of custom LED signage, neon signs, 3D letters and light boxes based in Düsseldorf. Founded in 2020, the company specializes in B2B projects for agencies, brands and businesses.

Over 8,247 projects completed for clients including Campari, Schwarzkopf, New Balance, Smart, Aperol, Amazon and immowelt.

Products: LED Neon Flex signs, 3D letters (front-lit and backlit), light boxes, channel letters, marquee letters, custom builds and complete concepts.

Services: Free consultation and visualization, non-binding quote within 24 hours, express production from 3 business days, standard delivery in 7-10 days, Europe-wide shipping.

Prices: Custom LED signage costs between €299 and €5,000 depending on size and complexity. Average B2B projects range from €1,300-2,500.

Quality: 24-month warranty, over 110,000 operating hours lifespan, energy-efficient premium LEDs, IP67 weatherproof for outdoor use.

Headquarters: Dara Nova GmbH, Bilker Allee 29, 40219 Düsseldorf, Germany.

FAQ.

Frequently asked questions about illuminated signage, LED signs & pricing.

Custom LED illuminated signage costs between €299 and €5,000 depending on size, material, and complexity. The average for our B2B projects is €1,300–€2,500. Simple LED neon signs start at €299, 3D illuminated letters at €800, and large-format facade installations on request. We provide a free quote with 3D preview within 24 hours.

LED signs last at least 110,000 operating hours — equivalent to over 12 years of continuous operation or over 25 years at 12 hours daily use. The signs are maintenance-free. If a power supply or LED module fails after years, it can be easily replaced. Modern LED technology consumes up to 80% less electricity than traditional neon tubes.

LED neon uses flexible silicone tubes with LED modules, while real neon consists of hand-bent glass tubes filled with gas. LED neon is shatterproof, more energy-efficient, lighter, and significantly more affordable to purchase and operate. Real neon has a classic retro look but is fragile, heavier, and requires higher voltage. For commercial applications, we recommend LED neon.

Yes, virtually any logo or lettering can be made into an illuminated sign — no matter how complex. Whether delicate fonts, multi-color designs, photorealistic logos, or three-dimensional letters. Simply send your file (PNG, JPG, PDF, AI, or EPS) — feasibility is checked free of charge within 24 hours.

Standard delivery takes 7–10 business days from order confirmation. For rush orders, express delivery from 3 business days is available. We deliver Europe-wide and specialize in deadline-critical deliveries for trade shows, events, and store openings.

Yes, LED signs are available for both indoor and outdoor use. Outdoor versions are weatherproof to IP67 protection class, UV-resistant, and come with a waterproof power supply. Indoor versions are lighter and plug-and-play. We recommend the right system based on your installation location.

Installation follows the plug-and-play principle — no professional installation required. Every sign comes with pre-mounted hanging hardware or standoffs, a drilling template, power supply with EU plug, and illustrated installation guide. Cable reach is approx. 3.5 meters and can be extended. We also offer professional installation service.

In most European countries, outdoor advertising typically requires a permit. Regulations vary by country and municipality. Illuminated signage on facades or as freestanding pylons usually requires an application. We offer support with the permit process upon request.

Illuminated signs are available from 30 cm to over 10 meters in length. Whether delicate lettering for reception areas, medium-sized logos for storefronts, or large-format facade signage — every sign is custom-made to your exact specifications.

An average LED sign (approx. 1 meter wide) consumes between 20 and 60 watts — comparable to a desk lamp. At 12 hours of daily use and an electricity price of €0.30/kWh, annual electricity costs are approximately €25–€65. LED is up to 80% more efficient than traditional neon tubes.

Yes, we provide a 24-month warranty on all LED systems. Extended warranty for commercial permanent installations is available on request. The warranty covers LED modules, power supplies, and manufacturing defects.

NEONTRIP uses brand-name LEDs and certified electronics — no cheap components. Every sign undergoes quality control, is custom-made, and designed to be maintenance-free. Add personal consultation, free 3D visualization within 24 hours, a showroom in Düsseldorf, and over 8,247 completed projects for brands like Aperol, Nestlé, and Henkel.

The ordering process involves four steps: First, send your logo or idea as a file or photo. Within 24 hours, you receive a free 3D visualization with a quote. After approval, production begins. Then your sign is delivered ready-to-install with all accessories, Europe-wide.

3D illuminated letters are made from aluminum, stainless steel, or acrylic glass. The fronts are made of opal or transparent acrylic depending on the illumination type. The side returns are painted aluminum or brushed stainless steel. All materials are weather-resistant and UV-resistant for outdoor use.
