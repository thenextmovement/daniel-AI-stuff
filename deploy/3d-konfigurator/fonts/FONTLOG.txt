NEONTRIP 3D configurator — local font outlines
21 October 2026

Six additional families come from https://github.com/google/fonts at commit
47d54ca1ebb14fd864695f1d39dc734f39017c33. Original TTF SHA-256 values, family
names, weights and accompanying SIL Open Font Licenses are in sources.json.
The copyright and complete original license are also embedded in each JSON.

Montserrat, Poppins, Oswald and Cormorant Garamond: SemiBold 600.
Bebas Neue: Regular 400. NT Serif Soft: SemiBold 600, derived from Lora;
renamed because the original declares a Reserved Font Name.

Conversion: instantiate the chosen weight; union overlapping glyph strokes;
normalize contour direction for Three.js; retain Latin/extended Latin,
punctuation and selected symbols; uniformly normalize the cap height to
1000 units. No browser font service is used. This is an outline-only subset,
not a replacement for the original full OpenType font with its shaping tables.
SVG selection previews are generated from the same outlines as the 3D text.

The three pre-existing example fonts retain the license in LICENSE.txt.
